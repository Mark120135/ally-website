隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-09-12

Ally 是一個住在 LINE 與 WhatsApp 裡的個人 AI 助理。它會動你的行事曆與文件—— 正因為如此，你有權知道它拿得到什麼、拿不到什麼、東西放在哪裡、以及怎麼一次全部收回。 這一頁講的就是這些，沒有法務黑話。

誰在處理你的資料

Ally 由個人開發者營運，聯絡方式：likemark1022@gmail.com。 任何隱私權問題、資料查詢或刪除請求，寄到這個信箱都會收到。

Google 使用者資料（重點段落）

連結 Google 帳號是選用的。不連結，Ally 的待辦與專案功能照常運作。 你如果連結，我們只會請求下面這些權限，每一個都對應一個你用得到的功能：

權限範圍Ally 用它做什麼它不能做什麼
calendar.events 查你今天／這週的行程；在你逐項確認之後，建立或修改事件 不會在你沒確認之前寫入任何東西
calendar.calendarlist.readonly 列出你有哪幾本日曆，才能把工作／家庭／學校的行程一起算進來 不能新增、刪除或改動日曆本身
calendar.settings.readonly 讀你 Google 帳號設定的時區，讓「明天早上十點」落在對的時間 不能更改任何設定
drive.file 只存取你親手在 Google 選擇器裡挑的檔案，或 Ally 幫你建立的檔案 看不到你雲端硬碟裡的其他任何檔案
documents / spreadsheets 讀取與編輯上面那些文件／試算表的內容（編輯前一定先讓你看到要改什麼） 碰不到你沒挑過的檔案
forms.body 依你的描述建立表單、加題目 不會幫你把表單發給任何人
forms.responses.readonly 讀取表單收到的回覆，整理成你看得懂的統計 不能代你回覆或修改別人的填答

我們刻意不要求 Google 日曆的完整權限，也不要求能瀏覽整個雲端硬碟的 權限——那些權限做得到的事（建立／刪除日曆、修改日曆共用設定、翻你所有檔案），Ally 一件都不做。

這些資料放在哪裡、放多久

  • 我們不保存你 Google 內容的副本。行事曆事件、文件內容、表單回覆都是 在你提出要求的當下即時讀取，用完就丟。
  • 文件摘要／圖片辨識／語音轉錄過程中的暫存內容，存活 30 至 60 分鐘， 到期自動刪除。
  • 長期保存的只有：你連結過的檔案識別碼、檔名、類型（這樣你下次說 「那份報名表」時 Ally 找得到），以及加密後的授權憑證。
  • 授權憑證加密儲存，而且在系統設計上絕不會被放進送往 AI 模型的內容裡 ——需要用到權杖的動作一律由後端代行。

Limited Use 聲明

Ally 對於從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與轉移，均遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策， 包含其中的 Limited Use 要求。

具體而言：從 Google Workspace API 取得的資料不會被用於開發、改進或訓練 任何一般化的 AI／機器學習模型；不會用於廣告；不會出售； 也不會提供給人類閱讀，除非取得你的明確同意、為了資安目的、或法律要求。

你的訊息會經過哪些服務

要讀懂你講的話，Ally 需要把訊息內容送給 AI 模型處理。經手的服務如下：

服務做什麼會拿到什麼
LINE / Meta（WhatsApp）訊息收發你與 Ally 之間的訊息

送往 AI 模型的每一次請求，我們都在請求本身帶上「不得留存、不得用於訓練」的 政策旗標——不是只在後台勾一個設定，而是寫在每一次呼叫裡，並且有自動化測試盯著它不准被拿掉。

我們不會出售你的資料，不會用於廣告投放，也不會把你的資料 提供給上表以外的第三方。

資料保留與刪除

  • 對話紀錄：只保留最近的 50 則往返，更舊的自動刪除。
  • 你建立的內容：保留到你刪除它、或刪除帳號為止。
  • 暫存檔案：30–60 分鐘後自動清除。
  • 整個帳號：在對話中輸入「刪除我的帳號」，Ally 會告訴你將發生什麼， 並要你一字不差再輸入一次作為確認。執行時會：
    1. 先向 Google 撤銷 Ally 的授權；
    2. 永久刪除你的全部資料——待辦、專案、對話、快取、稽核紀錄，全部，無法復原。
    刪除後只留下一筆不含任何個人資料的匿名計數紀錄。

你也可以隨時到 Google 帳號的第三方應用程式設定 單獨撤銷 Google 授權——撤銷之後 Ally 就再也碰不到你的行事曆與文件，其他功能照常。

你的清單連結

Ally 會給你一個唯讀的清單網址（/v/…）讓你在瀏覽器裡看自己的待辦。 這個網址不需要登入，安全性來自那串無法猜測的隨機字元（256 位元）。 它是唯讀的——任何人就算拿到連結也只能看，不能改任何東西。請把它當成密碼看待，不要公開轉貼。

安全措施

  • Google 授權憑證加密儲存，且不會進入送往 AI 模型的內容。
  • 資料庫層開啟 Row Level Security，每一次查詢都綁定你的租戶，不同使用者的資料在資料庫層就隔離。
  • 所有有副作用的動作（寫入行事曆、編輯文件、刪除資料）都要你先確認才執行。
  • 從外部讀回來的內容（文件、網頁、圖片）一律視為「資料」而非「指令」，不會被當成命令執行。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-12

Ally is a personal AI assistant that lives inside LINE and WhatsApp. It acts on your calendar and your documents — which is exactly why you deserve a plain answer to: what can it reach, what can it never reach, where does anything live, and how do you take it all back at once.

Who handles your data

Ally is operated by an independent developer. Contact: likemark1022@gmail.com. Privacy questions, data access requests and deletion requests all reach a human at that address.

Google user data (the section that matters)

Connecting a Google account is optional — Ally's to-do and project features work without it. If you do connect, we request only the scopes below, each tied to a feature you can use:

We deliberately do not request full Google Calendar access, and do not request any scope that can browse your whole Drive. Everything those broader scopes allow — creating or deleting calendars, changing sharing settings, reading files you never chose — Ally simply does not do.

Where this data lives, and for how long

  • We keep no copy of your Google content. Calendar events, document content and form responses are read live at the moment you ask, used, and discarded.
  • Intermediate content during summarisation, image recognition or transcription lives for 30 to 60 minutes and is then deleted automatically.
  • The only things kept long-term are: the ID, name and type of files you linked (so "that sign-up form" still resolves next week), and your encrypted credentials.
  • Credentials are encrypted at rest and, by design, are never placed into anything sent to an AI model — every call that needs a token is made by the backend on your behalf.

Limited Use disclosure

Ally's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: data obtained from Google Workspace APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI and/or machine-learning models; is not used for advertising; is not sold; and is not read by humans except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or where required by law.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties beyond the table above.

Retention and deletion

  • Conversation history: only the 50 most recent turns are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Content you created: kept until you delete it, or delete your account.
  • Files in transit: purged after 30–60 minutes.
  • The whole account: type "刪除我的帳號" ("delete my account") in the chat. Ally explains exactly what will happen and requires you to type the phrase again, character for character, to confirm. On execution it:
    1. revokes Ally's authorisation with Google first, then
    2. permanently deletes all of your data — to-dos, projects, conversations, caches and audit records — with no recovery.
    All that remains is an anonymous counter row containing no personal data.

You can also revoke Google access on its own at any time from your Google account's third-party apps settings. After that Ally can no longer reach your calendar or documents; everything else keeps working.

Your list link

Ally gives you a read-only web address (/v/…) for viewing your own list in a browser. That address requires no login; its security comes from an unguessable 256-bit random token. It is read-only — anyone holding the link can look, but can change nothing. Treat it like a password and don't post it publicly.

Security

  • Google credentials are encrypted at rest and never enter AI model input.
  • Row Level Security is enabled at the database layer; every query is bound to your tenant, so users' data is isolated in the database itself.
  • Every side-effecting action — writing to your calendar, editing a document, deleting data — requires your confirmation first.
  • Content fetched from outside (documents, pages, images) is treated as data, never as instructions, and is never executed as a command.

Children

Ally is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

International transfers

Ally's servers and database are located in the United States and Singapore, and the processors above may handle data in other countries. By using Ally you understand that data crosses borders.

If this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page; for material changes Ally will tell you in the chat.
